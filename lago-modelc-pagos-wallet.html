One wallet authority, one invoice authority

OwnerResponsibility
PagosAvailable/held balances, source grants, monthly reset/expiry, restricted/shared eligibility, quote versions, operation/component deduplication, ledger, refunds’ credit clawbacks, alerts, top-up thresholds/caps, reporting and recovery.
LagoProduct subscription billing, agreed billing calendar, invoice lines, proration, coupons/trials, billable usage aggregation and collection requests. Native paid features require the appropriate commercial edition.
StripePayment methods, PaymentIntents, actual collection, refunds and disputes. Use Lago invoice IDs as reconciliation references; do not expect a matching Stripe Billing subscription/invoice.
Product teamsApproved packaging and prices, actual provider usage facts and SDK integration. Wallet arithmetic and independent billing ledgers stay centralized.

Prevent double billing: a paid $100 credit purchase grants credits in Pagos once. Spending $10 reduces the Pagos wallet and records consumption; it does not generate another $10 Lago charge. Only expressly permitted overage or separate fees become billable Lago usage.

Prevent mistaken grants: an invoice can include several products, discounts and financial credits. The cancelled Alpha plan’s credit offset Architect’s invoice in our test. Calculate product grants from the approved entitlement and period policy, and deduplicate invoice/fee sources; do not equate cash collected with the allowance granted.

04 / BEFORE COMMITTING

What remains to build or qualify

  1. Choose the finance boundary: Premium for native manual credit notes/refunds, previews, emails and dunning, or explicitly own the missing workflows. Direct Stripe refund must reconcile cash, invoice correction and Pagos credit clawback.
  2. Build production Pagos: authoritative wallet, durable grant inbox/outbox, central SDK, frozen quotes, ambiguous response recovery, operation/component deduplication and reconciliation. The SQLite fixture is evidence for the integration contract only.
  3. Approve policies: calendar vs anniversary alignment, prorated credit grants, discounts, paid/failed renewal behavior, automatic financial credits and whether they may offset another product. The tested calendar behavior is not an automatic acceptance of every policy.
  4. Qualify operations: load and backlog latency, durable Redis/queues, failover, restore, version upgrades and full subscription/balance migration. Worker pause and restart checks are only a local subset.
  5. Validate paid/browser flows if selected: Premium features were not exercised with a license; generated checkout/portal links do not establish 3DS completion or the final customer-facing experience.
Full validation limits
  • No Premium license was available: gated Premium flows were denied/audited, not enabled or validated.
  • Renewals used normal Lago billing services/jobs at explicit timestamps, not Stripe Billing subscriptions or Stripe test clocks. Native Stripe sandbox PaymentIntents were observed succeeded.
  • Calendar billing in UTC was chosen. Initial invoices prorated: $3.87 Alpha and $7.74 Architect; three-day-later Architect starts at $5.81. Annual plan aligns to January 1. Anniversary alignment and other timezones need separate qualification.
  • The $30 base plus Pagos overage is the agreed scoped commitment, not a general native minimum-spend true-up engine.
  • The grant inbox and sales/consumption ledger are validation-only SQLite fixtures, not production Pagos implementation.
  • Checkout and portal URLs generated; browser checkout/3DS challenge completion not exercised in this Lago suite.
  • Tax/PO/UPI, arbitrary annual commitments, production load, HA/failover, coordinated restore, full migration and end-to-end SDK integration not qualified.
  • Manually refunding Stripe does not create a Lago credit note; custom refunds require an approved accounting and reconciliation process.
  • Invoice PDF download verified as PDF bytes after correcting stale public tunnel URL and enabling local HTTP settings. Private webhook URLs allowed only by local Compose override.
  • Wallet grant policy for prorated, discounted or credit-offset invoices must be specified by Pagos; money collected is not automatically the amount of product credit entitlement.
Decision: with the wallet deliberately in Pagos, Lago is a credible billing-engine choice without source forks. Community meets the tested core flows with the listed alternatives; it does not meet the complete native finance workflow. Obtain a Premium quote if that completeness is required, then test the licensed paths. The earlier Autumn-wallet recommendation addressed a different responsibility split.
