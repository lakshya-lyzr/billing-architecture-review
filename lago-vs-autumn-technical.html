Choose Autumn if reducing custom finance work is the priority.

With the wallet in Pagos, both engines support the tested core billing model. Autumn’s unpatched billing-only stack also passed native invoice previews, customer price overrides and manual credit-note/refund workflows that are Premium-gated in Lago Community.

Accept the tradeoff explicitly

Autumn retains the heavier tested infrastructure and needs a Pagos-to-Stripe final-overage adapter in this strict design. Lago Community has the simpler basic deployment but leaves additional finance workflows or a commercial license decision.

Neither option removes our wallet, usage ledger, SDK or reconciliation work.

The agreed Model C split

One organization payer, shared credits across products, independent product SaaS plans, consolidated recurring billing and separate immediate top-up/proration invoices. Pagos owns spendable credits and detailed cost/usage records. Separate sales and consumption reports are the launch policy; no recognized-revenue model is claimed.

Before production

  1. Implement and qualify the production Pagos wallet: atomic reservations, funding periods/expiry, ledger invariants and recovery.
  2. Build the shared API/SDK, immutable quotes, operation/component IDs, durable outbox/inbox and reconciliation.
  3. Approve grant, refund/clawback, discount/proration, failed-renewal and late-billing policies. Invoice cash collected is not automatically the product credit grant.
  4. For Autumn, qualify the subscription-scoped overage adapter, enforce empty credit/default configuration, and configure Stripe recovery/email policies.
  5. For Lago Community, own the missing finance workflows or validate a Premium license before relying on native credit notes, previews, emails or dunning.
  6. Qualify deployment capacity, failover/restore, notification delivery and real migration; shadow one product before collection cutover.

Lago’s hosting advantage

The documented basic topology is easier to package and operate. If operational simplicity or invoice ownership becomes the dominant requirement, Lago deserves another look.

Autumn’s hosting condition

Before adopting, name an owner for the deployment recipe and qualify timers, queues, replay and restores outside the local emulators. Accept the infrastructure cost explicitly.
